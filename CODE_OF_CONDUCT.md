# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for all people, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or socioeconomic status.

We expect all participants in our community — contributors, maintainers, and users — to treat each other with respect and create an environment where everyone can participate fully, safely, and comfortably.

---

## Expected Behavior

- **Be respectful** — Treat all people with dignity. Disagreement is healthy; hostility is not.
- **Be inclusive** — Actively welcome people of all backgrounds and perspectives.
- **Be constructive** — Offer feedback that helps improve ideas, not people. Focus on the work, not the person.
- **Listen actively** — Seek to understand other viewpoints before responding.
- **Ask before assuming** — If you're unsure what someone meant, ask for clarification.
- **Acknowledge mistakes** — If you harm someone, apologize genuinely and try to make it right.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not tolerated in any context:

- **Harassment** — Including unwelcome comments about identity, body, appearance, or personal choices; sustained disruption of discussions; threats or intimidation.
- **Discrimination** — Treating people unfairly based on any protected characteristic or personal attribute.
- **Hate speech** — Slurs, dehumanizing language, or content promoting violence against any group.
- **Sexual harassment** — Unwelcome sexual comments, advances, requests for sexual favors, or other forms of sexual conduct.
- **Doxxing or privacy violations** — Sharing someone's private information without consent (email, phone, home address, etc.).
- **Bad-faith arguments** — Deliberately misrepresenting someone's words or position to discredit them.
- **Spam or abuse** — Flooding discussions, off-topic promotions, or coordinated harassment campaigns.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- All GitHub repositories under the db-mcp-gateway organization
- All official communication channels (issues, pull requests, discussions, wikis)
- Official events, video calls, or chat channels associated with this project
- When representing the project in public spaces (e.g., conferences, social media)

---

## Reporting Violations

If you witness or experience a violation of this Code of Conduct:

1. **Email security@developerz.ai** with:
   - A description of what happened
   - The date and time (approximate is fine)
   - Names or usernames of those involved
   - Links to any relevant conversations or issues
   - Your name (optional if you prefer anonymity)

2. **What to expect:**
   - **Acknowledgment** within 48 hours
   - **Assessment** of the report (we will discuss severity and next steps)
   - **Confidentiality** — We will not publicly identify reporters without consent
   - **Action** — Depending on severity, this may include:
     - A private conversation with the person involved
     - A public warning
     - Temporary or permanent ban from the community

---

## Enforcement Guidelines

### Warnings

**First violation (minor):** A private message explaining the issue and the expected behavior.

Examples:
- A single off-topic comment in a discussion
- Tone that could be read as dismissive, but likely unintentional
- A misunderstanding quickly resolved

### Temporary Restrictions

**Repeated violations or serious conduct:** Temporary removal from community spaces (24 hours to 30 days).

Examples:
- Repeated off-topic posts after a warning
- Deliberately bad-faith arguments
- Sustained disruptive behavior

### Permanent Ban

**Severe or repeated violations:** Permanent ban from all community spaces.

Examples:
- Harassment, hate speech, or threats
- Doxxing or privacy violations
- Pattern of violations after warnings or temporary restrictions

---

## Appeals

If you believe a decision was made in error:

1. Email security@developerz.ai with your appeal and a detailed explanation.
2. A different maintainer will review your appeal.
3. We will respond within 7 days.
